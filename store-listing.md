# Formal Exchange — store listing copy

## English subtitle

Formal tickets, swaps and verified college exchange

## English promotional text

Find permitted Formal places, post buyer requests and make direct ticket-for-ticket swaps across Oxford and Cambridge—verified community, clear rules, safe messaging.

## English full description

Formal tickets can be extremely hard to obtain, yet plans change. A person who secured a place may later be unable to attend, while official refund or permitted resale routes are often limited. Cross-college and Oxford–Cambridge opportunities are also scattered across disconnected groups.

Formal Exchange brings permitted supply and demand together in one verified community marketplace.

VERIFIED COMMUNITY — TRUST AND SAFETY

Register with any email address. Users who verify with a UK academic (.ac.uk) email receive a visible verified badge; Oxford and Cambridge verified accounts can also publish Formal tickets. This verification system is designed to prevent fraud and keep out unauthorized users — only people with genuine academic connections can list Formal tickets. Our verification scope will expand to enterprise and global educational emails in the future; currently we serve UK institutions.

Why we verify:
- Prevents scams and unauthorized reselling.
- Ensures only genuine Oxford and Cambridge students list Formal tickets.
- Verified badge builds buyer confidence.
- Audience controls, automatic expiry, reporting and moderation add further protection.

TICKET SWAP — A CORE FEATURE

Have a ticket for College A but want College B? Find sellers who are open to exchanging one eligible ticket for another without requiring a cash sale. Whether to exchange directly or account for a price difference is a private decision between both members.

FORMAL FIRST

- Browse detailed Formal listings and post buyer requests.
- See college, date, time, place type, price, remaining availability, entry requirements, guest-name deadlines and transfer restrictions.
- Sellers decide visibility according to college rules: host-college members, other Oxford or Cambridge members, or wider users only where permitted.
- Only verified Oxford or Cambridge institutional accounts may publish Formal tickets.

MORE USEFUL CAMPUS EXCHANGE

- Coach and train tickets with departure, arrival and calculated journey time.
- Event places and airport ride-shares.
- College visiting records plus five-dimensional Formal ratings and comments.
- In-app conversations linked to the listing that started them.
- Chinese and English interfaces, with optional user text translated for readers using the other language and the original still available.

SAFETY AND RESPONSIBILITY

College and operator rules always come first. A prohibited transfer must not be listed or sold, and a seller must not knowingly sell to an ineligible buyer. Confirmed serious or repeated breaches may lead to removal, loss of Formal-listing access or account suspension.

Formal Exchange uses email verification with verified badges, audience controls, automatic expiry, reporting, moderation and private in-app messaging to reduce risk. The platform does not currently process or protect payments, guarantee admission or provide automatic refunds. Verify eligibility, evidence, price and transfer arrangements independently; never share passwords or verification codes; preserve messages and report suspicious conduct.

Website: formal-exchange.co.uk

认证社区——信任与安全

使用任意邮箱即可注册。使用英国高校（.ac.uk）邮箱认证后将获得可见的认证标识；认证的牛津和剑桥账号还可以发布 Formal 票。该认证机制旨在防止诈骗、杜绝闲杂人等混入——只有具有真实学术身份的用户才能发布 Formal 票。我们的认证范围今后将扩展到企业和全球教育邮箱；目前服务英国高校。

为什么要认证：
- 防止诈骗和未授权转售。
- 确保只有真正的牛津和剑桥学生可以发布 Formal 票。
- 认证标识增强买家信心。
- 可见范围控制、到期自动下架、举报和管理审核提供进一步保障。

换票专区——核心功能

手里有 A 学院的票，却想参加 B 学院？你可以筛选愿意直接用一张合规票交换另一张合规票的用户，不必先进行现金买卖。是否直接交换或补差价，由双方私下决定。

主营 Formal

- 浏览详细 Formal 帖子并发布买家需求。

- 只有已验证的牛津或剑桥机构账号可以发布 Formal 票。

安全与责任

学院和运营商规则始终优先。禁止转让的票不得发布或出售，卖家也不得明知买家不符合资格仍然出售。严重或重复违规经核实后，可能导致帖子下架、Formal 发布权限取消或账号暂停。

平台通过邮箱认证（含认证标识）、可见范围控制、到期自动下架、举报、管理审核和应用内私信来降低风险。目前平台不处理或保障付款、不保证入场，也不提供自动退款。请独立核验资格、票务证据、价格及转让安排；绝不要分享密码或验证码；保留消息并举报可疑行为。
